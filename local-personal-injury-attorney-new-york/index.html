What a claim may cover and how it proceeds

A claim can involve gathering records, reviewing the circumstances of an injury, identifying potentially responsible parties, and communicating with others involved in the matter. The process and result depend on the specific facts. These key points can help you organize an early conversation:

  • What it covers: Garber Legal handles vehicle accidents, falls, construction-related injuries, and medical malpractice matters.
  • Typical timing: There is no single schedule for every claim. The steps can depend on the available evidence, the course of treatment, and whether the matter is resolved through negotiations or proceeds to trial.
  • Cost considerations: The fee arrangement and responsibility for case expenses can differ. Ask for a clear explanation of how fees are calculated, which expenses may arise, and when they are paid. No specific price can be assumed without discussing the arrangement.
  • Terms you may hear: “Liability” refers to legal responsibility. “Damages” means losses a person seeks to recover. A “contingency fee” is a fee structure tied to a recovery, but you should confirm the exact terms in any agreement.

Bring any documents you have, such as incident details or medical records, and ask what else would help the attorney assess the matter. You do not need to know every legal term before asking for an explanation.
