What a personal injury attorney does

A personal injury attorney represents people seeking compensation after an injury they believe was caused by someone else’s actions or failure to act. The attorney reviews the circumstances, explains possible legal steps, and can handle discussions or legal proceedings on a client’s behalf. Each matter depends on its own facts, so an initial conversation is a chance to understand what may apply to your situation.

A personal injury attorney New York residents speak with may handle claims involving automobile accidents, slip and falls, construction mishaps, or medical malpractice. The type of incident matters because the records, people involved, and questions to investigate can differ.
